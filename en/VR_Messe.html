Virtual and augmented reality at trade fairs

Innovative trade fair experiences with VR and AR.

Virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) offer completely new possibilities for presentations at trade fairs. VR allows visitors to completely immerse themselves in a digital world and experience interactive experiences, while AR makes it possible to integrate digital content into the real environment. Both technologies create impressive, immersive experiences that present your products or services in a way that surpasses conventional methods.

By using VR and AR at trade fairs, you can inspire your visitors by presenting complex products and services in a simple and understandable way. AR is ideal for enhancing physical products with additional information or animations, while VR creates a completely digital environment in which your customers can try out your offerings virtually.

Thanks to our partnership with frontline.io we can offer customized VR and AR experiences for your trade fair presentations. Frontline.io offers powerful tools that allow us to create interactive and immersive presentations. With VR and AR, you can not only impress visually, but also offer interactive training and support in real time - directly at your trade fair.
