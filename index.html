About Us

GF Innovative Solutions is a German-based AI consultancy and implementation partner specializing in helping startups and SMEs unlock the real capabilities of artificial intelligence.

Here's what we see every day: companies are either overwhelmed by AI or underestimating what it can actually do for them. The gap between AI's potential and its current use in most businesses is enormous. Our mission is to close that gap.

We don't just consult. We implement. We build AI systems that automate operations, reduce costs, and free up your team for higher-value work. From custom AI agents to process automation and secure infrastructure, we handle both the strategy and the code.

We also offer immersive solutions like VR training and digital twins for industrial clients, particularly for trade fairs and remote demonstrations.

Made in Germany. Built for businesses that want results, not presentations.

AI implementation, not just strategy

Most companies underestimate what AI can actually do for them. They see chatbots and think that's it. But AI can automate entire operational workflows, process thousands of documents in seconds, and handle complex decision-making tasks that currently eat up your team's time. We don't sell you on the AI hype. We show you what's possible today, with your data, in your context. Then we build it. And here's the key: behind every AI system we implement, there's a human team monitoring, adjusting, and ensuring you get the results you need. That combination of AI power and human oversight is what makes it work.

Security and compliance built-in

Data protection isn't an afterthought for us. Every AI system we build is designed with GDPR compliance and German data protection standards in mind. We work with secure hosting providers, implement proper access controls, and ensure your sensitive data never leaves your control. Whether you need on-premise deployment, EU-hosted cloud solutions, or hybrid architectures, we design systems that meet your security requirements without sacrificing functionality. Because AI is only useful if you can trust it with your data.

Product meets people

Our edge is human. We're engineers who understand business, and business people who understand technology. One of us thinks in systems and architecture, the other in workflows and user needs. Together, we transform AI potential into practical tools your team will actually use. We can't guarantee that every AI experiment will work perfectly on the first try. But we can guarantee that there's a dedicated team behind every implementation, monitoring performance, fixing issues, and iterating until it delivers real value. That's the perfect combination of AI capabilities and human commitment.

From concept to continuity.

Fractional CTO & AI Strategy

  • Architecture design and code review
  • MVP development guidance

MOST POPULAR

AI Implementation Sprint

You know what process needs automation. We build a working AI solution in 4–8 weeks, test it with real data, measure the impact, and help you scale it.

  • Process analysis and requirements gathering
  • Custom AI system development (agents, automation, workflows)
  • Integration with your existing tools
  • Real data testing and validation
  • Performance monitoring and iteration

Perfect for: SMEs and startups ready to automate specific operational processes and see ROI quickly.

FOR GROWING COMPANIES

AI Infrastructure & Custom Agents

You need AI capabilities but want to own the infrastructure. We build and deploy custom AI agents, set up secure servers, and create automation workflows that run on your terms.

  • Custom AI agent development (document processing, customer support, data analysis)
  • Secure server setup and deployment (on-premise or EU-hosted cloud)
  • Integration with your existing systems (CRM, ERP, databases)
  • Workflow automation and orchestration
  • Ongoing monitoring and maintenance

Perfect for: Companies that need enterprise-grade AI solutions with full control over data and infrastructure.
