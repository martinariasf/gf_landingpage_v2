Our Product

The AI Marketing Platform

Built for marketing agencies and in-house marketing teams. Every client's posts live in one content calendar. Your AI agent drafts the copy and the visuals, you review them, and the client approves with a single link — no email threads, no "is this the final version?".

It is the same stack we run our own marketing on. The two reels below are the two halves of the workflow.

Step 1 · Create

Message your agent, get a finished post

Ask on WhatsApp or Telegram in plain language. The agent writes the caption, generates the image, and hands back a draft ready to schedule. Drafting that took hours takes minutes.

Step 2 · Approve

One calendar, one link, approved

The client opens one link and swipes through the month — approve, comment, or request a change. Every decision is logged against the post, so nobody chases a thread to find the latest version.

Multi-client workspaces

One login, every client separated. Roles for your team, your client, and read-only stakeholders.

Scheduling & publishing

Approved posts go out on schedule to the connected channels. Nothing is published without a human yes.

Your brand, not a template

The agent works from your brand assets, tone and past posts, so drafts arrive sounding like you.

Hosted in the EU

Runs on private European infrastructure under the same AVV and GDPR terms as the rest of our work.
