The problem

What the platform solves

Three things every content agency recognises, and what changes when the repetitive part runs itself.

Hours lost to repetitive content work

Writing posts, resizing, scheduling, assembling client documents, chasing validation. Every cycle, for every client.

From ~4.5 hours to ~12 minutes per cycle

The repetitive 80% of content work, automated end to end. Your team keeps the 20% that actually needs a human.

Client knowledge scattered everywhere

Email, WhatsApp, Drive, meeting notes, old drafts, feedback rounds, seven versions of the same file.

The Brain: one place per client

Every brief, asset, decision and past post in one searchable memory per client. Nothing gets lost when someone is on holiday.

Chaotic client ↔ agency communication

Many voices, contradicting each other across three channels. Nobody is sure what was approved, or by whom.

One channel, one explicit approval step

Everything the client needs sits in one place, with a clear approve-or-comment step. What they actually want is readable at a glance.
